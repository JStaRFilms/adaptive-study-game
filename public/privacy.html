Privacy policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

JStar Study is operated by JStaR Films. This policy explains how we handle information when you use our study tools. For privacy questions or requests, email johnoke2005@gmail.com.

Information used by the app

  • Study materials you choose to provide, including notes, documents, video links, prompts and tutor messages.
  • Study sets, generated content, quiz results, progress and preferences saved in your browser.
  • When Google sign-in is enabled, basic account information such as your name, email address, profile image and Google account identifier. Authentication may also store session and linked-account records. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Technical information needed to operate the service, such as request timestamps, browser information, network information, errors and AI usage counts.

Local storage and cookies

The app uses IndexedDB and localStorage to keep study data and settings on your device. This is not a cloud backup, and clearing site data or changing browsers can remove access to it. Local data is not encrypted by the app; someone with access to your browser profile may be able to read it. Where sign-in is enabled, cookies maintain your session. Signing out does not necessarily erase local study data.

AI processing

When you request AI features, the material needed for that request is sent to an AI provider. Depending on the feature and deployed version, this may include Google Gemini or OpenRouter and its selected model provider. Prompts, source content and relevant tutor conversation can leave your device. Video and search features may send links or queries to Google services.

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Their retention and model-training rules can vary by service and configuration. Do not upload passwords, financial information, private medical records or material you do not have permission to share.

Why we process information

We use information to generate study content, provide tutoring, save your progress, authenticate accounts where available, enforce usage limits, troubleshoot failures and protect the service from abuse. We do not sell your personal information or use Google account information for advertising.

Service providers and analytics

Vercel hosts the app. Vercel Analytics and Speed Insights collect website usage and performance measurements. Hosting providers may process request metadata and operational logs. When server-side accounts are enabled, Neon Postgres stores authentication, account and usage records; study sets remain browser-local rather than syncing to Neon.

Relevant provider policies: Google, OpenRouter, Vercel and Neon. Providers may process information outside your country.

Retention and your choices

Browser-local data stays until you delete it through available app controls or clear the site's data in your browser. Export a backup first if you want to keep it. Server-side account and usage information may be retained while your account is active and as needed for security, legal obligations and service operation. We do not currently promise a fixed retention period.

You can stop using AI features, revoke Google access through your Google account settings, or contact us to request access, correction or deletion of account information. Revoking Google access does not itself erase records already stored by the app. We may need to verify your identity before handling a request. Depending on your location, you may have additional rights, including the right to complain to a data-protection authority.

Children and security

The service is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no service or browser storage is completely secure.

Policy changes

We may update this policy as the app changes. The date above identifies the latest revision. Material changes will be noted on this page.
